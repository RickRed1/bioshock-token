## 1. Abstract
ADAM Token is a utility and governance digital asset deployed on the Polygon (POS) blockchain network. Drawing conceptual and visual design inspiration from 1960s Art Deco architecture and dystopian lore, ADAM Token bridges decentralized smart contract infrastructure with tokenized community engagement mechanisms.
